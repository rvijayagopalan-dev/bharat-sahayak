# BharatSahayak

**One Nation. One Connected Intelligence Ecosystem.**

BharatSahayak is a proposed national ecosystem of specialized, interoperable Sahayak platforms designed to connect citizens, government institutions, communities, businesses, researchers and service providers through shared intelligence, services and trusted collaboration.

> **Status:** Concept demonstrator (V1). This repository is not an official Government of India platform and is not connected to live government systems or datasets.

## Demo

Open [`demo/index.html`](demo/index.html) in a modern web browser.

The demo is a standalone HTML application. It does not require a build step, package installation, backend service or external API connection.

## Portfolio at a glance

The concept organizes 32 proposed platforms into seven national mission clusters:

| Mission cluster | Proposed platforms |
|---|---:|
| Citizen & Human Development | 10 |
| Agriculture & Rural Prosperity | 4 |
| Economy, Industry & Innovation | 5 |
| National Security & Resilience | 3 |
| Infrastructure, Environment & Mobility | 6 |
| Governance, Justice & Work | 3 |
| Culture, Tourism & Global India | 1 |
| **Total** | **32** |

The portfolio includes JanSahayak, SwasthyaSahayak, ShikshaSahayak, KaushalSahayak, KisanSahayak, EnterpriseSahayak, InnovateSahayak, VigyanSahayak, RakshaSahayak, GovSahayak and other proposed domain platforms.

## Design principles

1. **Federation by design** — participating institutions retain their mandates, operational authority and data ownership.
2. **Interoperability first** — connect existing systems through documented APIs, open standards and governed data exchange.
3. **Privacy and purpose limitation** — exchange only the data necessary for a defined and authorized purpose.
4. **Human accountability** — consequential, statutory and professional decisions remain with authorized people.
5. **Security separation** — apply appropriate identity, classification, least-privilege, encryption and audit controls.
6. **Inclusive access** — design for Indian languages, accessibility, low-bandwidth environments and assisted service delivery.
7. **Outcome orientation** — measure public value and real-world outcomes, not just platform activity.

## Reference architecture

The conceptual architecture contains:

1. Experience and access layer
2. Specialized Sahayak product layer
3. Bharat Intelligence Fabric
4. AI agent and workflow mesh
5. Shared digital services
6. Federated integration fabric
7. Existing systems and data owners

A cross-cutting trust, security and governance plane applies to every layer.

## Important limitations

- All dashboard figures, examples and journeys in the demo are illustrative.
- No live government, identity, health, financial, defence, agriculture or other institutional data is connected.
- The Sahayak names and portfolio groupings are proposed product concepts, not official government entities or endorsements.
- Any real deployment would require institutional sponsorship, legal authority, public consultation, privacy and security assessments, data-sharing agreements, procurement and operational ownership.
- Sensitive, classified and regulated information must remain in appropriately authorized environments.

## Roadmap

- **V1 — Portfolio demonstrator:** national overview, 32-platform directory, mission clusters, journeys, intelligence fabric and architecture.
- **V2 — Product deep dives:** richer domain capability maps, service catalogues, stakeholder ecosystems, workflows, knowledge models and command centres for each Sahayak.
- **V3 — Technical foundation:** reference APIs, domain ontology specifications, identity and consent patterns, agent governance and integration contracts.
- **V4 — Pilot blueprint:** select sponsoring institutions and a small number of measurable, non-sensitive service journeys.
