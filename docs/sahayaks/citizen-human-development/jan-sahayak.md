# JanSahayak

**Domain:** Citizen services  
**Mission cluster:** Citizen & Human Development  
**Status:** Proposed concept (V1)

## Mission

Unified discovery and assisted access to public services and entitlements.

## Stakeholders

- Citizens
- service centres
- public agencies

## Proposed capabilities

- Service discovery
- eligibility guidance
- application tracking

## AI agent concepts

- Service navigator
- eligibility assistant

## Proposed workspace modules

- Unified citizen workspace
- service catalogue
- case tracking

## Intended outcome

Human development.

## Implementation considerations

- Require institutional sponsorship and clearly assigned operational ownership.
- Apply privacy, purpose limitation, security, accessibility and human-oversight controls.
- Use federated integration with existing systems rather than centralizing sensitive data unnecessarily.
- Measure real-world outcomes through approved, non-sensitive indicators.

> This is a proposed product concept and is not an official government platform or endorsement.
